Sathira Silva

Research Engineer

I am a Research Engineer in the Computer Vision Department at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, working with Dr. Muhammad Haris Khan.

My research interests are in vision-language models, self-supervised learning, and 3D perception. I work on adapting vision-language models without target annotations, learning grounded representations from child-view video, and reliable visual recognition under domain shift.

I received my B.Sc. (Hons) in Computer Engineering from the University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka.

Current research

SafeMaize

Reliable maize screening under domain shift

I am investigating how visual recognition systems behave when field conditions obscure diagnostic evidence or unfamiliar symptoms appear. This work focuses on cross-source recognition, calibration, and selective prediction, alongside the development of MaizeShift-RBench.

I contributed to the technical research plan and methodology of the successful SafeMaize proposal, funded by the Gates Foundation through IAAI. I also contribute to field-data collection planning, agronomic annotation, and expert validation with project partners.
